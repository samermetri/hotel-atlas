A LITTLE CLARITY

Know your hotel credits.

For U.S. personal Amex Platinum and Chase Sapphire Reserve. Statement credits and benefits used at the hotel are different perks.

Amex Platinum

Up to $600 per calendar year, split into up to $300 January–June and $300 July–December. Eligible prepaid FHR or The Hotel Collection bookings must be made through Amex Travel using your eligible card. The Hotel Collection requires at least two nights. Starting January 1, 2027, FHR bookings also require two nights to qualify for the lodging credit.

Official Amex terms ↗

Chase The Edit

Up to $500 per calendar year, with a maximum of $250 per qualifying transaction. Requires a prepaid stay of at least two nights through The Edit in Chase Travel.

Official Chase benefits ↗

Chase Select Hotels · 2026

A separate credit of up to $250 for eligible prepaid Chase Travel bookings of two nights or more. Current terms cover purchases through December 31, 2026 at participating IHG, Montage, Pendry, Omni, Virgin, Minor, and Pan Pacific hotels.

“Select candidate” here means a directory listing has a matching Chase brand. It is an inference, not verified eligibility or a promise that credits will stack. Confirm the applicable credit in your Chase account.

Sources & coverage

Only Amex FHR, The Hotel Collection, and The Edit participation is included. The Select filter finds brand matches within these hotels; it does not include every Select Hotels property. This is a fixed snapshot, not an automatically updated directory. Different source names or locations may leave duplicate properties or missed overlaps.

All listings are third-party reported. “Amex + Chase” means a hotel is listed in both programs, not that one booking receives credits from both cards. Always verify participation and your remaining credit with the issuer before booking.
